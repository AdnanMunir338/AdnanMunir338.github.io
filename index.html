Adnan Munir

Hi! I am a doctoral researcher in Digital Image Forensics and Computer Vision. My research focuses on Vision-Language Models (VLMs), deepfake forensics, and developing robust multi-scale architectures to expose, localize, and explain subtle image manipulations.

Currently, I am pursuing my PhD in the Department of Electrical Engineering (ISY), Information Coding Division at Linköping University in Sweden, funded by the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program (WASP).

Prior to this, I was a Research Assistant at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM) where I earned my M.S. in Computer Engineering, and a Computer Vision R&D Engineer at E-Wall.
