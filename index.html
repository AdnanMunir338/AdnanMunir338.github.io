Adnan Munir

Hi! I am a doctoral researcher in Digital Image Forensics and Computer Vision. My research focuses on Vision-Language Models (VLMs), deepfake forensics, and developing robust multi-scale architectures to expose, localize, and explain subtle image manipulations.

Currently, I am pursuing my PhD in the Department of Electrical Engineering (ISY), Information Coding Division at Linköping University in Sweden, fully funded by the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program (WASP), where I also serve as a Teaching Assistant.

Prior to joining Linköping University, I spent 2021–2025 as a Graduate Research & Teaching Assistant at the King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM), Saudi Arabia, and worked as a Computer Vision R&D Engineer at E-Wall.
